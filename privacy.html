Privacy policy

TrueNAS Backup is used only by its owner, Hyatt Baker, to back up his own files to his own Google Drive.

  • It accesses Google Drive solely to upload, update and remove the owner's backup files.
  • It collects no personal data, has no other users, and shares nothing with any third party.
  • Access credentials are stored only on the owner's private home server and can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings.
